Common questions

Will the AI ever post something without me?

Only what you allow on the Auto-reply page. New posts wait for your approval unless you change that. You can pause everything with one button.

What if the AI gives a wrong price?

It can't. Replies that mention a price are blocked unless the price matches your product list.

Is my Facebook password stored?

No. You log in on Facebook's own page. We keep an encrypted access key that you can remove any time.

Why can't I reply to TikTok comments here?

TikTok doesn't let other apps reply to comments yet. Posting and results work.

Still stuck?

Write to us in Bangla or English. We answer within a few hours, 10 AM to 10 PM.
